About Us

Great Food, Live Beats,
& the Ultimate Beach Vibe

Situated right opposite Novotel Dona Sylvia Resort in Mobor, Cavelossim, Mike’s Place is a multi-cuisine restaurant specializing in authentic Goan dishes and fresh seafood, paired with vibrant live music. Food & fun for everyone!

Whether it’s breakfast, lunch, dinner, or a fun night out, Mike’s Place is where good times always find a table. Celebrate birthdays, anniversaries, and life’s milestone events right here in South Goa.
